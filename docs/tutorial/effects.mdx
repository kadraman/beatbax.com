# Effects

Finish **Tutorial Groove** with named `effect` presets and inline note modifiers. Availability can vary by chip — see [Sound Chip Plugins](/docs/chips/overview).

## Named presets and inline use

```bax
effect wobble = vib:6,4
effect spark  = arp:4,7
effect slide  = port:16

pat lead_c    = G5 . A5 C6<wobble>:2 A5 G5 E5 .
pat harmony_a = C4<spark>:4 E4:4 G4<spark>:4 E4:4
pat bass_b    = C3 . E3<slide> . G2 . . G2
```

**Arpeggio (`arp`):** list only steps **above** the root — do not include `0`.

You can also attach a preset as a sequence modifier: `seq lead = melody:wobble`.
