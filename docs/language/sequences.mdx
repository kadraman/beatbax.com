# Sequences

A **sequence** (`seq`) is an ordered playlist of pattern (or nested sequence) references. Where a `pat` is one phrase, a `seq` chains several phrases over time.

```bax
seq name = <SeqRhs>
```

Empty sequences (`seq name =` with nothing after `=`) are **errors** — every `seq` needs at least one reference.

## Sequence RHS \{#sequence-rhs\}

### Pattern references \{#pat-refs\}

| Form | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| `patName` | Play that pattern once |
| `name:mod(…)` | Apply [modifiers](/docs/language/modifiers#modifier-catalog) (chainable) |

### Repeats (`*N`) \{#repeat\}

| Form | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| `patName*N` | Repeat the reference `N` times |

### Groups \{#groups\}

| Form | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| `(a b)*N` | Group and repeat |

```bax
pat lead_a = E5 . G5 C6 . G5 E5 .
pat lead_b = C5 . E5 G5 . E5 C5 .

seq lead_seq  = lead_a lead_b*2
seq drums_seq = (drums_a drums_b)*2
seq lifted    = lead_a:oct(+1):rot(2)
```

Items may be separated by spaces or commas. Comments (`#` / `//`) are allowed between items.
