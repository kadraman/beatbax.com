# Notes and lengths

Pattern tokens describe pitch and timing. The grammar recognizes note tokens, rests/sustains, and an optional duration suffix (`:N` or `/N`).

## Note tokens

| Form | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| `C4`, `E5`, `G#5`, `Bb3` | Letter `A–G`, optional sharp (`#`) or flat (`b`), then octave. Optional hyphen before the octave (`C-4`) is accepted. |
| Default length | **1 step** when no duration suffix is present |

A **step** is one timing unit at the song’s master `bpm`.

```bax
pat phrase = C4 E4 G4 C5
```

## Lengths

Append `:N` or `/N` (same meaning) to hold a note for **N** steps:

| Form | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| `C4:4` | Hold C4 for 4 steps |
| `G5/8` | Hold G5 for 8 steps |

Duration attaches to the preceding pattern atom (note, rest, or other token).

## Rests and sustains

| Token | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| `.` | Rest — silence; also cuts the previous note |
| `_` or `-` | Sustain — keep the previous note sounding for one more step |

`C4 _ _ _` is equivalent in length to `C4:4`.

```bax
pat held = C3:16
pat groove = E5 . G5 C6 . G5 E5 .
pat sustain = C4 _ _ _
```
